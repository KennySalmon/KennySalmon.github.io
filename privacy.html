Privacy policy

Draft for Kenneth's review. Last updated October 23, 2026.

This draft describes the current static site and the Formspree endpoint shown in the contact form. Kenneth must confirm the form service, destination inbox, retention schedule and legal wording before relying on this policy.

Information collected

  • Site visits: GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Requests also reveal technical details such as the requested page or file, time, browser and device information. Kenneth does not receive or control GitHub's security logs.
  • Contact inquiries: the form asks for your name, email address, service type, project deadline and message. The deadline is optional. The form also sends a subject line and a blank honeypot field used to screen automated submissions. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.
  • Video thumbnails and players: video thumbnails are hosted on this site. Viewing a thumbnail does not send a request to Google or YouTube. Video players load only after you choose a video. The showreel uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced player domain. Google or YouTube may receive player, device and interaction data and may use cookies or similar technologies after a player is activated.
  • Uploads: this site has no file upload field.
  • Analytics, cookies and AI: no analytics service is currently active. The site itself does not set analytics cookies or use local storage. A Cloudflare Web Analytics token placeholder is present, but it loads nothing until Kenneth configures a real token. This site has no AI feature, and visitor information is not sent to an AI system.

Who receives information

  • GitHub Pages serves the site and collects security logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Formspree receives form fields and request details at the endpoint currently present in the site code. It may process submissions for delivery, storage and spam prevention. Kenneth has not verified the active Formspree account, plan or destination inbox. See Formspree's plan and archive details.
  • Google and YouTube serve the embedded player after a visitor chooses to play a video. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gmail receives and stores inquiry notification emails if Formspree delivers them to Kenneth's listed Gmail address. Email sent directly by a visitor is also handled by the visitor's and Kenneth's email providers.
  • Instagram and LinkedIn receive information only if you follow those outbound links.

The site does not currently use Turnstile, an analytics beacon, an advertising pixel, a review widget or an AI service.

Retention and deletion requests

Kenneth proposes to keep inquiry messages for no longer than 24 months after the last contact, then remove them from the Formspree submissions area and Gmail inbox and trash. Formspree's currently listed free plan archives submissions for 30 days, but Kenneth must confirm the account plan and settings. If you ask for earlier deletion, email carlossalmon77@gmail.com with the email address used in your inquiry. Kenneth will locate and delete the inquiry from the services he controls within 30 days and request help from the provider if needed.

GitHub controls its security logs and each service controls its own backup copies and deletion timing. Kenneth cannot directly erase GitHub logs or guarantee removal from provider backups. There are no visitor uploads or site-owned backup copies of inquiry data. Kenneth must confirm this process with the selected form and email providers before launch.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact carlossalmon77@gmail.com. This draft should be reviewed whenever the form, video embeds or analytics change.
